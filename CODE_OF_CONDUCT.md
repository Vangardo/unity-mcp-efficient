# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberate disruption are not.

Examples of useful participation include:

- reporting a reproducible problem without exposing private project data;
- explaining tradeoffs and benchmark conditions;
- reviewing ideas on their technical merits;
- helping newcomers understand the project without talking down to them.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces managed by the project. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on protecting a productive community.

For a sensitive conduct report, use a private [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/Vangardo/unity-mcp-efficient/security/advisories/new) and state that the report concerns community conduct. Do not publish personal or sensitive details in an issue.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect the reporter's privacy where possible, and disclose conflicts of interest before another maintainer handles the case.
